## 4. Architectural Purpose
WP-GOV-01C-EXT establishes the mechanism for cryptographically sealing qualitative and quantitative evidence provided during a governance evaluation. Its purpose is to ensure that the parameters defining a synthetic evaluation provision are verifiably locked to the specific state of evidence at the time the provision is generated, ensuring non-repudiation and auditability without polluting the broader traceability graph.

## 5. ProvisionScope.parameters Contract
The implementation ensures that `ProvisionScope` explicitly includes a robust `parameters` JSON payload containing the structured evidence evaluated by the synthesis engine. This contract forms the basis for subsequent event-driven evaluations and allows the governance layer to introspect exactly what evidence was utilized.

## 6. evidenceSnapshotHash Contract
The implementation introduces `evidenceSnapshotHash` to the core schema. This cryptographic hash is computed strictly from the content within the `ProvisionScope.parameters` and acts as a localized seal for the evaluation evidence, guaranteeing its integrity independently from other platform-wide hashes.

## 7. Separation from traceabilityGraphHash
The `evidenceSnapshotHash` has been architecturally decoupled from the `traceabilityGraphHash`. This separation ensures that the `traceabilityGraphHash` maintains its role in mapping the structural lineage of artifacts and entities, while the `evidenceSnapshotHash` acts purely as a content seal for the specific evaluation inputs at a point in time.

## 8. Deterministic Canonicalization
To prevent identical evidence sets from producing divergent hashes due to JSON key ordering, the implementation utilizes strict, deterministic JSON canonicalization (e.g., standardizing key sorts and string encoding) before computing the `evidenceSnapshotHash`.

## 9. Infrastructure-ID Exclusion
The canonicalization process rigorously filters out transient, infrastructure-specific identifiers (e.g., UUIDs generated by the database for outbox events or transient row IDs). This ensures that the hash strictly reflects the substantive evidence rather than the database topology or temporal state.
